# Project Elevator Pitch

Elevator Pitch
LocalWinAI is your personal, on‑device AI engine — a fast, private, and deeply integrated assistant powered entirely by your computer’s NPU. It brings the intelligence of modern language models directly into Windows, running locally with zero cloud dependency, zero latency, and zero data leaving your machine.

At its core, LocalWinAI transforms your PC into a self‑contained AI workstation. It chats, summarizes, classifies, embeds, analyzes, and automates — all accelerated by the Intel NPU and optimized for real‑time interaction. As the ecosystem grows, LocalWinAI becomes more than a chatbot: it becomes a local AI operating layer, capable of powering apps, tools, and workflows across your system.

With upcoming MCP integration, LocalWinAI evolves into a local AI coprocessor for tools like Claude Code. Instead of sending every task to the cloud, Claude can delegate small jobs — summaries, embeddings, code checks, heuristics — directly to LocalWinAI, letting your NPU handle the work instantly and privately.

LocalWinAI is the future of personal AI:
local, fast, private, extensible, and always available — the AI that truly belongs to you.
